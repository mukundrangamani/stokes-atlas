Primary roots and horizons

Planar primary labels follow the root formula in Expiscating the black hole singularity in holography. For a global complex pair j₀ is the upper member in the squared-radius plane; for an exterior real pair it is the larger root. Global d = 6 uses j₁ for the negative real root. RN uses j₀,j₁ for the upper/lower complex pair and j₂ for the real root.

H₊ is the outer horizon and H₋ the RN inner horizon. Hc is the negative squared-radius RN horizon. In the neutral views H₁ (d = 4) and H₁,H₂ (d = 6) denote the other horizons; the latter form a complex-conjugate pair. For d = 4, H₁ lies at coordinate infinity.

Spheres and continuation

The spheres compactify the base coordinate, rather than the two-sheeted WKB spectral curve. The full and magnified views show the same finite-frequency core graph. Solid and faint dashed segments distinguish front and rear hemispheres.

The red contour Γ(t) = t − 0.025 i sin(πt) runs from B to H₊; it is a continuation contour, not a Stokes trajectory. Crossings C₁,C₂,… are ordered along it. General graphs use phases −0.08,0,+0.08; neutral core graphs additionally include ±0.02.
